ML-DSA Context-String and HashML-DSA Prehash Negative-Test Pack

Reusable evidence pack checking ML-DSA context-string and HashML-DSA handling in two pinned ML-DSA-44 implementations against FIPS 204 (DOI 10.6028/NIST.FIPS.204).

Headline results

  • Harness cases: 45, matched expected outcome: 45, mismatches: 0.
  • Valid-agreement cases: 21 VALID as expected; negative/error cases: 21 rejected or failed as expected; N/A probes: 2.
  • Context handling is fully conformant in both implementations: cross-verification liboqs ↔ dilithium reference is VALID in both directions with a non-empty context, and every wrong-context, overlong-context, altered-signature, message-mismatch and wrong-key negative is rejected.
  • HashML-DSA works in the dilithium reference for SHA-256, SHA-512 and SHAKE128-256, and its construction is independently confirmed by pyca/cryptography sign_mu/verify_mu (H-10, both directions VALID).
  • liboqs 0.16.0 exposes no HashML-DSA API (N/A, NA-1): prehash signing is unavailable there, not merely untested.
  • ACVP known-answer vectors: sigGen 10/10, sigVer 15/15, keyGen 25/25 (50/50).
